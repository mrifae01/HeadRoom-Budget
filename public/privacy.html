Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 2, 2026  ·  Last updated: April 2, 2026

Plain-English summary: HeadRoom helps you manage your budget. We collect only what we need to make the app work, we never sell your data, and you can delete everything at any time.

1. Who We Are

HeadRoom ("HeadRoom," "we," "us," or "our") is a personal budgeting application developed and operated by a sole developer based in the United States. You can reach us at mrifae@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information about you when you use HeadRoom at headroombudget.com or through our mobile applications (collectively, the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password. Authentication is handled securely by Supabase.

Budget and financial data you provide: HeadRoom stores the budget information you enter directly, including:

  • Income sources and amounts
  • Debt obligations and monthly payments
  • Spending categories and budget limits
  • Transactions you manually log
  • Savings goals and progress
  • Monthly financial records and summaries

Bank account data (if you choose to connect a bank): HeadRoom optionally integrates with Teller, a bank connectivity service, to import real transaction data. If you connect a bank account, we receive and store:

  • A secure access token issued by Teller (never your banking username or password)
  • Bank account names, types, and basic details
  • Transaction history (up to 90 recent transactions)
  • Institution name

Your banking credentials are entered directly into Teller's secure widget and are never transmitted to or stored by HeadRoom.

Usage data: We collect basic, anonymized usage analytics through Vercel Analytics, including page views and general usage patterns. This data does not include personal identifiers.

Communications: If you contact us for support or submit feedback, we may retain those messages to respond to you and improve the Service.

Waitlist email (if applicable): If the Service is at capacity, you may optionally provide your email to be notified when a spot becomes available. This email is used only for that notification and is not used for marketing.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service
  • Generate AI-powered budget analysis and advice using your budget context (via Anthropic's Claude API)
  • Import and display your bank transactions if you connect a bank account
  • Respond to your support requests and feedback
  • Notify you when a spot opens on the waitlist (waitlist emails only)
  • Understand how the Service is used in aggregate to make improvements

We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the Service for you.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. We share data only with the following service providers as necessary to operate HeadRoom:

  • Supabase — database hosting and authentication. Your account data and budget data are stored in Supabase's cloud infrastructure.
  • Teller — bank account connectivity. If you connect a bank, Teller processes your bank credentials and provides us with transaction data. Teller's use of your data is governed by Teller's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic — AI-powered budget analysis. When you use the AI assistant, a snapshot of your budget context (income, categories, debts, recent transactions) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. Anthropic's use of API data is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — web hosting and analytics. Anonymized usage data may be processed by Vercel.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from the Settings screen within the app. Upon deletion, all of your data — including budget records, transactions, monthly records, and bank connections — is permanently erased from our systems within a reasonable timeframe. Waitlist emails are retained until a spot is offered or you request removal.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS
  • Bank access tokens are stored server-side and never exposed to the frontend
  • Passwords are hashed by Supabase; we never store plaintext passwords
  • Row-level security policies ensure each user can only access their own data
  • API endpoints are authenticated and rate-limited

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to protecting your data to the best of our ability.

7. Your Rights (California Residents — CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know: You may request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: You may request that we delete your personal information. You can do this directly from Settings → Delete Account within the app.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell personal information.

To exercise these rights, contact us at mrifae@gmail.com. We will respond within 45 days.

8. Children's Privacy

HeadRoom is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

9. Links to Third-Party Services

The Service may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and this Privacy Policy does not apply to them.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Service after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
